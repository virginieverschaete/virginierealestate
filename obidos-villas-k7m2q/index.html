Contemporary villas in Óbidos

A small development of contemporary three-bedroom villas with a private pool, in Bairro da Senhora da Luz, Óbidos. Five minutes from the walled town of Óbidos and from Caldas da Rainha, close to the Óbidos Lagoon and the beaches of Foz do Arelho, and about an hour from Lisbon.

Each villa has three bedrooms and three bathrooms, an open living area with a fully equipped kitchen, a private garden and terrace, a barbecue area and covered parking. Built with the Plastbau system for thermal and acoustic insulation, with photovoltaic panels, solar hot water and pre-installation for air conditioning. Expected energy rating A+. You choose your finishes within a set allowance per category.

Payment plan

Payment plan

You buy the plot and sign a separate construction contract. Payments follow the progress of the works:

  • 1%Reservation
  • 4%Promissory contract (CPCV)
  • 20%Deed of the plot
  • 25%Structure complete
  • 20%Finishing phase 1: roof, wall finishes, floors, tiles and stone
  • 20%Finishing phase 2: painting, kitchen, bathrooms, joinery, electrical fittings, pool and exterior
  • 10%Habitation licence

Because the plot and the construction are bought separately, IMT and stamp duty are paid on the value of the plot (20% of the total price), not on the construction. Always confirm the taxes for your situation with your lawyer.
